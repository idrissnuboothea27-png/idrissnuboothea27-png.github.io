02Customer research

Communication pressure survey

Orange Réunion, August 2026
QuestionnaireAnalysisRecommendations
Role
Questionnaire design, build on Eloquant, SPSS analysis, report and recommendations.
Problem
Dashboards measure sends and clicks, not how customers perceive commercial pressure or their avoidance behaviour.
Approach
26 questions on a 1-to-10 scale, five blocks (profile, frequency, perception, usage, image), sent by SMS to 12,000 customers.
Results
Three more sensitive profiles (under 38, mobile-only, reached by e-mail alone) and an action plan: perceived-pressure indicators, frequency by profile, respect for the preferred channel.
